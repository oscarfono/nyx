* Buckets
| Bucket | Account | Rule                                |
|--------+---------+-------------------------------------|
| Income |         | Pay lands here                      |
| Bills  |         | Annual costs / pays per year        |
| Buffer |         | Fill to three months of costs, then stop |
| Spend  |         | Safe-to-spend × weeks per pay       |
| Invest |         | All surplus after the buffer        |

* Safe to spend
spend per week = (net per year − annual costs − buffer per year − invest per year) / 52
LUNA calculates the number from the Income, Annual costs and Targets tables.
