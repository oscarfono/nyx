#+TITLE: Roster

LUNA reads this file in code for the roster readout, the next swing,
and the morning brief. Keep the two tables in this form.

* Pattern
One row. The first on-swing of the pattern starts on the anchor date.
The shift changes each on-swing when Alternate is yes.

| Anchor     | On days | Off days | First shift | Alternate |
|------------+---------+----------+-------------+-----------|
| 2026-11-25 |      14 |       14 | nights      | yes       |

* Swings
A row here wins over the pattern for its dates: a changed swing, a
different site, the flights. Type is ON or OFF. Shift is nights or days.

| Start      | End        | Type | Shift  | Site | Fly from | Fly to | Notes |
|------------+------------+------+--------+------+----------+--------+-------|
